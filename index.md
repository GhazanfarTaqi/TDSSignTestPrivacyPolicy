# Privacy Policy for TDS Quiz App

**Last Updated: November 6, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to TDS Quiz App ("we," "us," or "our"). We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you use our mobile application, and how it is used.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information Stored on Your Device

- **Quiz responses and progress:** Your answers and scores are stored locally on your device only. We do not send them to our servers.
- **Language preference:** Your selected language (English or Urdu) is stored locally on your device.

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or phone number through the app.

### 2.2 Information Collected by Third-Party Services

The app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. When ads are shown, Google may automatically collect:

- Device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID)
- Approximate location (derived from your IP address)
- IP address
- Device and app information (device model, OS version, app version)
- Ad interaction and performance data
- Diagnostic information

We do not have access to this information. It is collected and processed by Google under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 3. How We Use Information

The app itself uses the information stored on your device only to provide quiz functionality (for example, remembering your language choice). Advertising data collected by Google AdMob is used to deliver, measure, and improve ads, which helps support the free availability of this app.

## 4. Advertising

This app uses Google AdMob to serve advertisements. AdMob may:

- Display banner ads within the app
- Show other ad formats, such as full-screen ads, at natural breaks in the app
- Use device information to deliver relevant ads and measure ad performance

**Your ad choices:** You can opt out of personalized ads or reset your advertising ID in your device settings:

- Android: **Settings → Google → Ads** (the exact path may vary by device)

## 5. Data Storage and Retention

- **Quiz data and language preference:** Stored locally on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.
- **Advertising data:** Held by Google according to its own retention policies.

## 6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share information with third parties, except:

- **Google AdMob:** the SDK in the app communicates with Google for ad delivery and measurement, as described above.
- **Legal compliance:** if required by law.

## 7. Children's Privacy

TDS Quiz App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information through the app, please contact us and we will take steps to address it.

## 8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the limited information handled by the app. Because quiz data stays on your device, its security also depends on the security of your device. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Your Choices

You can:

- Clear the app's data from your device settings
- Uninstall the app
- Opt out of personalized ads or reset your advertising ID in your device settings

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
